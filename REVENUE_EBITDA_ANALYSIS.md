# Revenue & EBITDA Analysis: Cummins Power Systems vs Caterpillar Energy & Transportation

## Executive Summary

This analysis compares the financial performance of **Cummins Power Systems** and **Caterpillar Energy & Transportation** segments from 2019-2024, based on data from investor presentations and quarterly earnings releases.

## Data Sources

- **Cummins Inc.**: Investor Relations earnings releases, segment reporting (2019-2024)
- **Caterpillar Inc.**: Investor Relations earnings releases, segment reporting (2019-2024)

**Note**: Caterpillar reports "segment profit" (operating profit) rather than EBITDA. Some 2019-2021 values were estimated from quarterly earnings data and annual segment reporting.

## Key Insights

### 1. Revenue Scale & Growth

**Scale Difference:**
- Caterpillar's Energy & Transportation segment is **~4.5x larger** than Cummins Power Systems (2024)
- CAT E&T 2024 revenue: **$28.9B**
- Cummins Power Systems 2024 revenue: **$6.4B**

**Growth Performance:**
- **Cummins Power Systems CAGR (2019-2024): 8.8%** - Higher growth rate
- **Caterpillar E&T CAGR (2019-2024): 6.7%** - Steadier growth from larger base

**Growth Trends:**
- Cummins showed exceptional growth in 2022-2024, with revenues increasing 52.6% over this period
- Both companies benefited from strong data center demand and power generation needs
- Caterpillar maintained more consistent growth from a significantly larger revenue base

### 2. Profitability Performance

**2024 Margins (Converging):**
- Cummins EBITDA Margin: **19.7%**
- Caterpillar Operating Margin: **19.9%**
- Margins now nearly identical despite different starting points

**Margin Expansion (2019-2024):**
- **Cummins: 5.2% → 19.7% (+14.5 percentage points)** - Dramatic improvement
- **Caterpillar: 10.0% → 19.9% (+9.9 percentage points)** - Strong improvement

**Key Drivers:**
- Pricing power in power generation equipment
- Operational efficiency improvements
- Strong demand for data center power solutions
- Mix shift toward higher-margin products

### 3. Profitability in Dollar Terms

**2024 EBITDA/Operating Profit:**
- Caterpillar: **$5.75B** (4.5x larger than Cummins)
- Cummins: **$1.27B**

**2019-2024 Profit Growth:**
- Cummins EBITDA grew **475%** (from $220M to $1,265M)
- Caterpillar Operating Profit grew **175%** (from $2,088M to $5,750M)

### 4. Business Momentum

**Cummins Power Systems:**
- Accelerating revenue growth: From negative growth in 2020 to 18.7% YoY in 2022
- Margin expansion trajectory steeper than Caterpillar
- Smaller but faster-growing segment with improving profitability

**Caterpillar Energy & Transportation:**
- More consistent revenue growth (9.8% to 15.3% range 2021-2023)
- Larger absolute dollar profits ($5.75B vs $1.27B in 2024)
- Maintained strong margins while growing from much larger base
- More mature market position with stable performance

## Market Context

### Key Market Drivers (2019-2024)

1. **Data Center Boom**: Explosive growth in AI and cloud computing driving power generation demand
2. **Energy Transition**: Backup power needs for renewable energy installations
3. **Industrial Recovery**: Post-COVID industrial activity rebound (2021-2022)
4. **Pricing Power**: Both companies successfully implemented price increases
5. **Supply Chain**: Improved supply chain conditions in 2023-2024

### Competitive Positioning

**Cummins Power Systems:**
- Strong position in distributed power generation
- Significant exposure to data center market
- Rapid margin improvement suggests operational excellence and market positioning gains

**Caterpillar Energy & Transportation:**
- Broader product portfolio (engines, turbines, generators, locomotives)
- Established market leader with larger installed base
- Diversified end markets provide stability

## Conclusion

Both companies have demonstrated exceptional performance from 2019-2024, with particularly strong results in 2022-2024:

- **Caterpillar E&T** maintains its position as the dominant player with ~$29B in revenue and strong, consistent profitability
- **Cummins Power Systems** has shown remarkable margin expansion (5.2% → 19.7%) and faster revenue growth (8.8% CAGR), emerging as a stronger competitor

By 2024, both segments achieved similar **~20% EBITDA/operating margins**, indicating operational excellence and strong market conditions in the power generation sector.

The data suggests a robust and growing power systems market with healthy competition driving operational improvements and innovation.
