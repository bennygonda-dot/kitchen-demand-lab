## 📊 Executive Summary

This analysis examines three major UK range cooker competitors using official Companies House financial data. BSH UK dominates with £791.2M in revenue (2023), while SMEG UK shows strong growth (+34%) and Rangemaster faces significant challenges (-20% revenue decline).

- **BSH UK**: £791.2M revenue, £24M profit, 81.4% market share (of analyzed companies)
- **SMEG UK**: £65M revenue, 6.7% market share, +34% growth
- **Rangemaster**: £115.5M revenue, £15.2M profit, 11.9% market share, -20% decline
