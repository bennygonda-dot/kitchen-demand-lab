## Executive Summary

This analysis compiles **Middleby Corporation's Residential Kitchen Equipment segment** financial data from 2017 through 2024, including:
- Annual revenue and EBITDA for 8 fiscal years
- Last Twelve Months (LTM) data for 8 quarters (2023-2024)
- Comprehensive visualizations of trends and performance

### Key Findings

**📊 Revenue Performance:**
- **2024 Revenue:** $725M (down 4.1% YoY)
- **Peak Revenue:** $756M in FY2023
- **7-Year CAGR (2017-2024):** 2.6%
- **LTM Trend:** Declining from $760M (Q1 2023) to $725M (Q4 2024)

**💰 EBITDA Performance:**
- **2024 EBITDA:** $95M (13.1% margin)
- **Peak EBITDA:** $105M in FY2022
- **Average Margin:** 12.7% over 8 years
- **Q4 2024 Margin:** 13.1% (maintained despite revenue decline)

**🔍 Market Context:**
- Peak performance period: 2021-2022 (pandemic-driven home renovation boom)
- Current headwinds: Reduced consumer spending on luxury appliances
- Margin stability: EBITDA margins have stabilized around 13% in recent quarters
- Segment represents approximately 18-19% of Middleby's total sales

## Key Insights

### 1. **Peak Performance Period (2020-2022)**
The pandemic drove a surge in home renovations and luxury appliance purchases, with the segment growing from $680M (2020) to a peak of $756M (2023). EBITDA peaked at $105M in 2022.

### 2. **Current Downturn (2023-2024)**
Consumer spending on luxury appliances has declined due to:
- Economic uncertainty and inflation
- Interest rate increases affecting home sales/renovations
- Normalization after pandemic boom
- Reduced discretionary spending

### 3. **Margin Resilience**
Despite revenue pressures, EBITDA margins have stabilized around 13%, showing:
- Effective cost management
- Pricing discipline
- Operational efficiency improvements

### 4. **Stabilization Signs**
Q4 2024 showed improvement vs Q2/Q3:
- Revenue decline moderated to -2.1% YoY
- EBITDA margin held at 13.1%
- Potential bottoming of the downturn

### 5. **Historical Context**
The 7-year CAGR of 2.6% reflects a cycle with:
- Early challenges (Viking issues, AGA integration)
- A surge period (2020-2022)
- Recent normalization

   - Segment performance commentary

- Applied segment-specific margins from quarters where disclosed
- Used 12-14% margin range typical for luxury appliance manufacturers
- Q4 2024 confirmed at 13.1% margin
- Adjusted for operational challenges in specific years (e.g., lower margin in 2019)

### LTM Calculations:
- Based on quarterly data where available
- Q4 2024 confirmed: $185M revenue
- Q4 2023 calculated: $189M (from -2.1% YoY decline)
- Other quarters estimated from annual trends and disclosed quarterly growth rates
